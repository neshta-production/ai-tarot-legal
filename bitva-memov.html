Битва мемов

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Битва мемов is a free word game with Russian and English localization. It includes one optional consumable in-app purchase that adds 100 energy. The App does not include advertising, subscriptions, user accounts, or tracking across apps and websites.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Битва мемов ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account, email address, phone number, location, date of birth, photo, or other personal information for gameplay. Limited technical and usage information may be processed through AppMetrica as described below. Information is also shared with us if you voluntarily send a support email.

2. Game Data

The App stores game state locally on your device, including settings, onboarding completion, sound and haptic preferences, scores, prizes, room items, energy balance, and the time used to restore energy. This local game state is not uploaded to a developer-operated account or game server.

3. Contact Us

If you choose “Contact Us,” the App opens your email client with a draft addressed to imslowpoke@gmail.com. The draft contains the message you choose to write and diagnostic details added by the App: your device model, iOS version, and App version. The App does not send the draft automatically. This information is shared with us only when you voluntarily choose to send the email.

4. Device Permissions

The App does not require access to sensitive device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, calendars, reminders, health data, Bluetooth, or photo library.

5. Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

The App does not track you across apps or websites and does not include third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, or targeted advertising. The App uses Yandex AppMetrica to understand app launches, sessions, feature usage, performance, and basic technical information such as App version, operating-system version, device characteristics, network information, and identifiers used for analytics and fraud prevention. This information is used to improve stability and gameplay and is not used by us to serve targeted advertising.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App is free to download and includes one optional consumable in-app purchase that adds 100 energy. Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment-card details. The App receives transaction information needed to verify the purchase and prevent the same transaction from being credited twice. Consumable energy cannot be restored after it has been used. The App does not include subscriptions.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been sent to us, contact us at imslowpoke@gmail.com so we can review and delete it.

8. Data Retention

Local app data can be removed by deleting the App from your device. Analytics information is retained according to our AppMetrica configuration and the service provider’s applicable retention practices. Support emails and included diagnostic details may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue, and maintain support records. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing imslowpoke@gmail.com.

9. Security

The App is designed to keep gameplay, energy, purchases credited to the game, and settings on device. Analytics and App Store transactions are handled through their respective service providers. You should keep your device and iOS version updated and protect access to your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its analytics, purchases, or other data handling changes. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at imslowpoke@gmail.com.
